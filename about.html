About me

Where I'm from, where I've lived, and what I'm up to now.

I grew up in northern New Jersey before moving to Atlanta, where I studied at Georgia Tech and graduated with BS in Industrial Engineering with Highest Honors.

For the past 6 years, I've lived in Chicago and New York, working in management consulting across the consumer and retail space and in business operations at a fintech startup. New York is also where I met and recently proposed to my fiancé, Sanjana.

Now, I’ve returned to the Windy City as a MBA/MPCS joint degree candidate at UChicago, seeking to pivot into the tech industry in a product or strategy oriented role. The work I enjoy the best and thrive in sits at the intersection of strategy, technology, and execution.

Outside of work, you'll usually find me trying out new recipes I come across on Instagram, looking for the perfect picture with my DSLR, playing/watching/talking tennis, or working on my latte art.
