About me

Where I'm from, where I've lived, and what I'm up to now.

I grew up in the suburbs of New York, in New Jersey, and moved to Atlanta for undergrad at Georgia Tech. Since then I've lived in Chicago and New York, and I'm back in Chicago now.

Right now I'm taking a pause from working to pursue an MBA at Chicago Booth and an MS in Computer Science at UChicago. The goal is to pivot into the tech industry, and I'm using these two years to build the skills and the network to do it.

Outside of work, you'll usually find me trying out new recipes I come across on Instagram, playing new board games with friends, or out with my camera.
